# Interview guide (5 to 6 respondents, one chosen segment)

Do not collect account numbers, PAN, or portfolio screenshots.

1. Why did you start your first SIP? What did you expect?
2. What has the experience been since?
3. Have you paused, reduced, or thought of stopping? What led to it?
4. What do you check, how often, and how do you feel about what you see?
5. When you feel unsure, what do you do outside the app?
6. What would make you more likely to continue?
