# Research guide

Used for the survey and any follow-up conversations with first-time SIP investors.
Do not collect account numbers, PAN or portfolio screenshots.

## Questions
1. Why did you start your first SIP, and what did you expect?
2. What has the experience been since?
3. Have you ever paused, lowered or thought of stopping? What led to that?
4. What do you check, how often, and how do you feel about what you see?
5. When you feel unsure, what do you do outside the app?
6. What would make you more likely to continue?

## MVP test task
"Imagine you want to pause your SIP. Try it and think aloud." Then ask:
- Did the note feel helpful, pushy or neutral?
- Did you trust it?
- What would you change?

## Survey summary (15 respondents, small convenience sample)
- 7 of 15 have paused or stopped their SIP. 5 of the 8 with under 6 months of tenure did.
- 8 of 15 cite market swings (4) or growth below expectations (4) as a reason for doubt.
- 3 of 15 cite expenses or uneven income.
- 0 of 15 contact in-app support when unsure. They use Google and friends (4 each), YouTube and Reddit (2 each).
- Choosing a fund and jargon are the top open complaints, which the MVP does not address.
