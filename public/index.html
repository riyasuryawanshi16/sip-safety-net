How the safety net works

  1. Ask why.
    The user picks a reason for pausing. No pressure, no guilt.
  2. Draft and check.
    AI drafts a short note. Rules and a second AI review it for advice, fund names, urgency and promises.
  3. Safe by default.
    If a check fails, a standard message is shown. Pausing is always one tap away.
